The story

Once upon a dream

Disney’s 1950 classic turned a weary hearth into a midnight ball — and a glass slipper into forever.

Kindness meets cruelty in a house of ashes, until a fairy godmother arrives with a pumpkin, mice, and a dress spun from starlight. One night is all she is granted — and one dance is all the prince needs.

This page is an unofficial fan celebration of that Disney fairy tale: romance, courage, and the idea that the right shoe can still find the right foot.

Magic

Three sparks of midnight

The charm of Cinderella lives in a handful of unforgettable images.

  • The glass slipper

    Fragile, luminous, and impossible to fake — proof that wonder leaves a footprint.

  • The pumpkin coach

    An ordinary gourd becomes a golden carriage, because magic loves a good transformation.

  • The stroke of twelve

    Midnight steals the gown, but not the memory — or the other slipper left behind.
