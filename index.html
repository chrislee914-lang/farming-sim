Ritual

A tin, used properly.

  1. 01

    Warm the tip

    Hold the balm against your lip for a second. It should give before it glides.

  2. 02

    One pass

    Sweep once across. A second pass is for wind, winter, or a long lecture.

  3. 03

    Pocket it

    The cap clicks. Keep it with your keys. Reapply when the sheen goes quiet.

The atelier

Bloom and Balm started as a kitchen kettle.

We wanted chapstick that smelled like the plant it named, not a candle aisle. Beeswax for hold, cocoa butter for slip, and a short list of flowers, peels, and honey. Nothing that numbs. Nothing that vanishes in an hour.

Every tin is 0.15 ounces, poured warm, and cooled on a rack by the window. If a batch smells shy, it does not ship.
